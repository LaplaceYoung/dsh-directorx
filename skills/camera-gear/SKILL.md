# Camera & Gear

Gear language is look language: describe shots as "shot on 35mm full frame, 85mm f/1.4, shallow DOF, handheld gimbal movement".

## Sensor and look
- Sensor size > resolution; dynamic range > pixel count; stability > spec sheet. Pro DR 13-15+ stops, entry 10-12 — DR drives the film look more than megapixels.
- Crop math: full frame 36×24; APS-C ×1.5 (35mm ≈ 52mm FF); M4/3 ×2 (25mm ≈ 50mm FF); 1" ×2.7. Small sensors fake full-frame with longer focal + more distance + wider aperture.
- Ask for Log-style flat renders when grading follows (gray footage is normal).

## Focal-length cheat sheet (FF equivalent)
- 14-24 ultra-wide (space/impact), 24-35 wide (environmental), 35 documentary standard, 50 human-eye universal, 85 portrait/close-up compression, 100-200 telephoto compression; 24-70 = interview workhorse.
- One lens for everything is a mistake: wide on faces distorts, tele on environments is too narrow.

## Exposure discipline
- Aperture locks depth of field first; shutter locked by the 180° rule (24fps→1/48s, 30fps→1/60s); control exposure with aperture + ISO + ND only — never shutter (killing motion blur = "video look").
- ND exists to keep shutter at 1/48-1/60 in daylight, not just to darken: fixed ND4/8/16/64 or variable (watch color cast + cross-pattern).
- Dual-native-ISO bodies pick the clean gain stage; cine lenses: T-stop = measured transmission (typical T1.4-T2.8).

## Stabilization ladder
Tripod (static; thick legs first) → monopod (sports/events) → gimbal (dynamic follow; balance first — unbalanced is worse than handheld; bent knees, small steps) → Steadicam (long complex moves) → shoulder (doc/war feel) → drone (aerial; plan route, no wind). OIS/IBIS as floor; post stabilization is rescue only (crop + warp + rolling shutter).

## Audio and feasibility
- Mic > camera: lavalier indoor, shotgun outdoor, condenser studio; always a backup recording.
- Feasibility checks in AI plans: night shoots need fast aperture + lights; drone needs airspace permission; indoor VO needs lavalier + soft furnishing (reverb control); long takes need an overheating plan.
- Lights and mics pay back more than body upgrades — budget tiers: <$5k = phone + light + mic; $5-15k = APS-C/FF mirrorless + 24-70 equiv + gimbal; >$30k = cinema body + cine lenses + lighting package.

## AI generation application
- Specify lens feel to avoid flat video look: wide-angle distortion, telephoto compression, shallow-DOF bokeh, motion blur ("shutter feel").
- Deliverable-oriented gear lists: body/lens/stabilization/lighting/audio + budget tier when asked.
