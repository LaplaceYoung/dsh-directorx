# Product Photography — Techniques Reference

15 product photography techniques with prompt templates, background taxonomy, material vocabulary, and visual signal guides.

## 15 Product Photography Techniques

### Technique 1: Ghost Mannequin / Invisible Mannequin

Apparel photographed on a mannequin that is composited out in post, leaving a 3D shape with no visible support.

**Prompt template:**
```
[garment] displayed on invisible mannequin, seamless white studio background, even studio lighting from both sides, collar and cuffs shaped naturally, 3D garment form maintained, no wrinkles on fabric face, commercial apparel photography standard
```

**When to use:** All e-commerce apparel listings, any garment where body shape improves perceived fit.

### Technique 2: Flat Lay

Product(s) photographed from directly overhead on a flat surface, often with styled props.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product(s)] flat lay, overhead birds-eye view, [surface material] surface, artfully arranged with [props], even diffused lighting, no shadows on product face, Pinterest-ready composition, negative space [left/right/bottom] for text placement
```

**Prop vocabulary:** `dried botanicals`, `raw linen napkin`, `minimal geometric shapes in complementary color`, `coffee/matcha mug nearby`, `journal and pen`, `no props, product alone`

**When to use:** Social posts, lifestyle content, food/beverage/wellness products.

### Technique 3: On-Model Lifestyle

Product worn or used by a real person in a relatable, realistic setting.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] worn by [person descriptor], [environment], [lighting], candid unposed moment, authentic lifestyle feel, product clearly visible but not staged-looking, [camera spec]
```

**Critical notes:**
- Specify whether the person is staged or candid: `posed and aware of camera` vs. `candid mid-activity moment`
- Always specify where the product is visible in frame: `product centered at hip level`, `product visible on wrist in foreground`

**When to use:** Apparel, accessories, beauty products, supplements in use.

### Technique 4: Hero Shot (Single Product)

Clean, dramatic presentation of one product with full attention on its form and details.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] hero shot, [surface], [lighting setup], slight tilt to show 3D form, product fills 60% of frame, crisp sharp focus on entire product, [background], commercial photography standard, no props
```

**Angles for hero shots:**
- `slight 3/4 angle, label facing camera` — standard for bottles/packaging
- `direct front facing, centered, symmetrical` — for flat products
- `slight bottom-up angle` — for tall premium products (creates prestige)
- `from above at 30-degree angle` — for wide products

**When to use:** Main listing image, hero banner, print ads.

### Technique 5: Detail / Macro Close-Up

Extreme close-up showing texture, material quality, or craftsmanship that larger shots miss.

**Prompt template:**
```
extreme macro close-up of [specific detail] on [product], 100mm macro lens, f/8, sharp detail render, [texture description] of material visible, studio softbox lighting to show surface texture, no depth of field blur on subject area
```

**Best details to capture:**
- `label printing texture and emboss detail`
- `fabric weave and fiber texture`
- `product surface material grain`
- `product edge finishing and seam quality`
- `product cap/closure mechanism`

**When to use:** Premium positioning, supplement detail images, apparel quality signals.

### Technique 6: Studio Three-Point Lighting

Classic commercial product setup with key light, fill light, and rim/separation light.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] on [surface], studio three-point lighting: key light from [left/right] at 45 degrees, soft fill from opposite side, rim light from behind creating separation from background, [background], commercial product photography standard, all product details clearly rendered
```

**When to use:** Default for any product where no specific mood is needed. The safest choice for client work.

### Technique 7: Natural Light Editorial

Product photographed in natural light with editorial styling — feels authentic and artisan.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] in natural light editorial style, [surface with texture], soft diffused daylight from window [left/right], light and airy atmosphere, slight shadow detail preserved, warm tones, organic and handcrafted feel, [props if any], no artificial lighting visible
```

**When to use:** Wellness brands, organic products, artisan/handcrafted positioning, food and beverage.

### Technique 8: Overhead / Birds-Eye

Looking straight down at the product — creates clean graphic compositions.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] overhead photography, perfect birds-eye view, [surface], even diffused overhead lighting, no shadow intrusion, geometric composition, [additional elements arranged around product], graphic and clean, suitable for social media
```

**When to use:** Flat products, beauty products arranged, food, tech accessories.

### Technique 9: 45-Degree Angle (Standard Commercial)

The workhorse of product photography — slightly above and to the side.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] shot from standard 45-degree angle, [surface], [lighting], product fills frame naturally, label/face of product toward camera, all key features visible in single frame, commercial e-commerce standard
```

**When to use:** Default for most e-commerce secondary images.

### Technique 10: Group / Collection Arrangement

Multiple products from the same line arranged together.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product line name] collection arranged together, [surface], [arrangement style: symmetrical/asymmetrical/cluster/row], [lighting], varying heights [if applicable], cohesive visual family, no single product dominates, [background]
```

**Arrangement styles:**
- `symmetrical row, equal spacing, same height` — clinical, systematic
- `asymmetrical cluster, overlapping slightly, varying depths` — natural, editorial
- `pyramid arrangement, hero product centered and tallest` — hierarchy emphasis

**When to use:** Collection launches, bundle promotions, brand overview imagery.

### Technique 11: In-Use / Action Shot

Product being actively used by a person — shows the product's purpose in the most direct way.

**Prompt template:**
```
[person descriptor] actively using [product], [specific action], [environment], [lighting], product clearly visible and in focus, candid action moment, not staged-looking, [camera spec]
```

**Action vocabulary by category:**
- Supplement: `pouring drops into water`, `holding dropper over glass`, `shaking bottle before use`
- Apparel: `walking confidently`, `mid-stride movement`, `reaching for something`, `sitting relaxed`
- Skincare: `applying to face with fingertips`, `holding up to light examining`, `opening cap`
- Tech: `typing with product nearby`, `glancing at screen`, `unpacking product`

**When to use:** Conversion-focused images, PDP secondary images, social content.

### Technique 12: Scale Reference

Shows product next to familiar object so viewer can accurately understand size.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] next to [scale reference object] for size comparison, [surface], [lighting], both items in sharp focus, product and reference item similar distance from camera, natural placement not forced, clean background
```

**Best scale references:** `human hand (open palm)`, `coffee mug`, `ruler visible at edge`, `coin (specify size)`, `known packaged item`

**When to use:** Any product where size is unclear from standard shots, tech accessories, supplements vs. finger for scale.

### Technique 13: White Seamless Background

The e-commerce standard — pure white, no distractions, pure product focus.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] on pure white seamless background, even studio lighting, no shadows on background, product clearly separated from background, Amazon product photography standard, [camera spec], all product details sharp
```

**Critical notes:**
- Include `pure white, no grey cast on background` — Nano Banana models sometimes render off-white
- Include `product clearly separated, defined edges` to prevent blending into background

**When to use:** Amazon listings, any marketplace requiring white background standard.

### Technique 14: Textured / Lifestyle Background

Replaces white with a surface that adds mood and brand personality.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] on [textured surface], [surface color/character], [lighting to show texture], texture visible in foreground and background, product elevated slightly above surface [if applicable], [style]
```

**Background surface taxonomy:**

**Solid:**
- `pure white seamless` — clinical, Amazon-standard
- `pure matte black` — premium, luxury, dark mode
- `warm sand gradient` — wellness, organic, clean beauty
- `cool grey gradient` — tech, minimal, modern
- `pastel sage green` — natural, wellness, calming

**Natural (textured):**
- `warm walnut wood grain` — handcrafted, premium, natural
- `white Carrara marble, subtle grey veining` — luxury, beauty, premium
- `rough concrete slab, industrial texture` — modern, urban, premium streetwear
- `natural linen textile surface, slight texture` — organic, wellness, artisan
- `river stone surface, smooth grey` — natural, grounding, wellness

**Lifestyle:**
- `kitchen counter, marble surface, morning light` — food, supplements, health
- `bathroom vanity, white tile, morning routine feel` — beauty, skincare
- `wooden desk surface, morning work setup` — productivity, tech, coffee
- `outdoor wooden table, natural light` — food, beverages, outdoor brands

**Abstract:**
- `soft gradient bokeh background, [primary color]` — lifestyle, beauty
- `textured paper surface, handmade feel` — artisan, small-batch, organic
- `gradient from [light] to [dark], [color family]` — modern, digital-native

### Technique 15: Reflective Surface

Product photographed on a surface that creates a perfect mirror-image reflection below.

**Prompt template:**
```
[product] on glossy [color] reflective surface, perfect mirror reflection below product, [lighting to enhance reflection], premium product photography, clean composition, [camera spec at low angle to capture both product and reflection]
```

**Notes:**
- Use `slight angle, low camera position` to capture the reflection properly
- Specify `clean unbroken reflection, no surface imperfections`
- Best surfaces: `black gloss acrylic`, `white gloss surface`, `polished dark marble`

**When to use:** Premium / luxury product positioning, perfume, spirits, tech accessories, watches.

## Luxury vs. Mass-Market Visual Signals

Use these signals intentionally to position the product at the right price point.

### Luxury Visual Signals
- Less product visible in frame — white space dominates
- Material texture given as much screen time as the product itself
- Lighting is moody, directional, not even
- Background is textured marble, dark surfaces, or stark black
- Camera angle is slightly dramatic — low angle or unusual perspective
- Props are zero or one, and high quality
- Color palette: `ecru`, `warm white`, `black`, `forest green`, `deep navy`, `champagne`

### Mass-Market Visual Signals
- Product large in frame, filling majority of space
- Background is clean white or soft gradient
- Lighting is even, bright, all details visible
- Multiple angles shown
- Lifestyle background is relatable (kitchen, bathroom)
- Color palette: bright, high-saturation, accessible

## Dynamic Elements Catalog

Add motion or life to product photography without people:

- `steam rising from [hot product]` — coffee, tea, supplements in warm water
- `water droplets on exterior surface` — freshness, cold temperature, purity
- `liquid being poured mid-action` — supplements, beverages, oils
- `product falling/floating` — creates energy, editorial feel
- `leaves/petals scattered around product` — natural, organic, botanical positioning
- `dry ice or smoke wisps at base` — luxury, mysterious, dramatic
- `light refracting through glass product` — premium glass packaging
- `fabric flowing naturally in slight breeze` — apparel, textiles
- `product emerging from packaging` — unboxing reveal
- `multiple exposures of same product, different angles` — technical, premium
